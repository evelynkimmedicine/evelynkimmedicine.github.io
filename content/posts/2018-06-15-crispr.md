The Potential of CRISPR/Cas9 Gene Editing as a Treatment Strategy for Alzheimer’s Disease. *Journal of Alzheimer's Disease & Parkinsonism*, 8(3\): 439\. [https://doi.org/10\.4172/2161\-0460\.1000439](https://doi.org/10.4172/2161-0460.1000439)



> "Targeting the genetic roots of both familial early\-onset and sporadic late\-onset Alzheimer's disease through programmable endonucleases offers a transformative paradigm beyond symptomatic management."


This early foundational publication explores how CRISPR/Cas9 genome editing can be engineered to correct pathogenic mutations in the amyloid precursor protein (*APP*) and presenilin (*PSEN1*, *PSEN2*) genes, as well as converting the high\-risk *APOE4* allele toward the neuroprotective *APOE2/APOE3* isoforms.


**Key Highlights & Translational Insights:**


- Evaluated viral and non\-viral central nervous system delivery vectors capable of traversing the blood\-brain barrier.
- Analyzed off\-target cleavage risks, mosaicism, and ethical considerations required before human clinical translation.
- Established the molecular foundation for Evelyn's subsequent undergraduate and graduate investigations into neurodegenerative therapeutics.
