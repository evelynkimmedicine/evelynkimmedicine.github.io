**Poster Citation:** Kim, N., Best, M.N., Lim, Y., Ferenc, N.N., & Bloom, G.S. (2022\). *Qualitative Analysis of BetaIV\-Spectrin Protein in the Axon Initial Segment.* Poster presented at the Virginia Section of the American Chemical Society (ACS) and University of Virginia Department of Chemistry Undergraduate Research Symposium.


BetaIV\-Spectrin anchors voltage\-gated sodium channels to the actin\-spectrin cytoskeleton within the Axon Initial Segment (AIS). In this study, we performed high\-resolution confocal imaging to evaluate how exposure to toxic extracellular tau oligomers fragments BetaIV\-Spectrin lattices in cultured neurons.
