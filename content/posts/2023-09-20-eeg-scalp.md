**Project Archive:** [Plotting EEG Sensors on the Scalp — Neuro\-Analysis Portfolio](https://medevelynkim.wordpress.com/neuro-analysis-projects/)


Accurate spatial registration of electroencephalography (EEG) electrodes is essential for source localization, seizure focus mapping in neurosurgery, and cognitive event\-related potential (ERP) studies.



**Technical Implementation Highlights:**

- Aligned standard 10\-20 and high\-density 64\-channel sensor montages with fiducial anatomical landmarks (Nasion, Inion, Left/Right Preauricular points).
- Rendered both 3D cranial surface sensor projections and 2D topographic contour maps for alpha/theta band power inspection.
- Explored clinical applications in pre\-surgical epilepsy monitoring and neurodegenerative slowing analysis.
