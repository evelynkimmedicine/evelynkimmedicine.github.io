**Presentation Citation:** Robertson, E., Kim, N., Campbell, M., Gabrieli, L., & Cornell, K. (2019\). *Developing Naturopathic Remedies and Novel Drugs to Combat Giardia intestinalis Infections.* Poster presented at the University of Idaho Engineering Design EXPO.


Addressing growing metronidazole resistance in global waterborne parasitic infections, our research team screened novel small\-molecule enzyme inhibitors alongside standardized botanical extracts for antiparasitic activity against *Giardia intestinalis*.


**Methodology & Outcomes:** Conducted *in vitro* susceptibility assays, IC50 dose\-response curves, and host mammalian cytotoxicity profiling to identify selective therapeutic candidates suitable for low\-resource clinical settings.
