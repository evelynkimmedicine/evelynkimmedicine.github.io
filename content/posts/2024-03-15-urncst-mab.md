Monoclonal Antibody Treatment Delivery for Alzheimer’s Disease & BBB. *The Undergraduate Research in Natural and Clinical Science and Technology (URNCST) Journal*, 8: A1–A5\. [https://doi.org/10\.26685/urncst.551](https://doi.org/10.26685/urncst.551)



> "Less than 0\.1% of systemically administered monoclonal antibodies typically cross the intact blood\-brain barrier—making receptor\-mediated transcytosis and focused ultrasound critical frontiers in Alzheimer's immunotherapy."


**Key Contributions:** As lead author, Evelyn evaluated emerging delivery strategies—including transferrin/insulin receptor\-mediated transcytosis (RMT), nanoparticle conjugation, and focused ultrasound—to improve central nervous system bioavailability while mitigating Amyloid\-Related Imaging Abnormalities (ARIA\-E and ARIA\-H).
