**Original Post Link:** EP3\. Staircases and Second Chances (October 30, 2024\)



> "A successful craniotomy or spinal decompression in the operating room is only half the battle; the other half is fought on the four flights of walk\-up stairs waiting at the patient's apartment."


By late October, I transitioned into leading independent psychosocial assessments on the neurosurgery unit. Through the stories of patients like Mrs. Pinkston, Mr. "J", and Mr. "R", I learned how environmental realities—like narrow New York City walk\-up staircases, living alone, or lacking home health coverage—directly dictate post\-surgical survival and recovery.


Advocating fiercely for placement in Acute Inpatient Rehabilitation rather than premature home discharge required synthesizing physical therapy evaluations, family dynamics, and insurance appeals while honoring each patient's dignity and self\-determination.
