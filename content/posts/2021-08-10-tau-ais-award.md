**Award & Citation:** Nayoung Kim, University of Virginia. *Is Tau Pathology Linked to Disruptions of the Axon Initial Segment?* Recognized as **The Histochemical Society 2021 Capstone Awardee**.



> "The Axon Initial Segment (AIS) serves as the structural and physiological gatekeeper of action potential initiation and neuronal polarity. When tau oligomers disrupt this barrier, neuronal signaling falters."


Using quantitative immunofluorescence microscopy and histochemical staining in primary cortical neuron cultures, this project investigated how pathological tau species alter key AIS scaffolding proteins, laying the groundwork for two subsequent peer\-reviewed papers in 2023\.
