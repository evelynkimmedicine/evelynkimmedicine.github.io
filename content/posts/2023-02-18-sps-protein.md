**Project Archive:** [Neuro\-Analysis & Structural Biology Projects](https://medevelynkim.wordpress.com/neuro-analysis-projects/)


Conducted an in\-depth three\-dimensional structural and functional analysis of **Sucrose\-Phosphate Synthase (SPS)** from the thermophilic cyanobacterium *Thermosynechococcus elongatus* using **ICM Browser (MolSoft LLC)**.


**Key Structural Findings:**


- Mapped the GT\-B glycosyltransferase fold comprising two Rossmann\-type β/α/β domains separated by a deep catalytic cleft.
- Inspected hydrogen\-bonding networks and conserved basic residues coordinating UDP\-glucose and fructose\-6\-phosphate substrate binding.
- Demonstrated how computational structural biology tools bridge molecular biochemistry with rational therapeutic design.
