> "When a pregnant patient in acute psychosis enters Labor & Delivery, the clinical team is literally holding two lives at once—and stigma can never be allowed in the room."


In this episode, I reflected on shadowing in Labor & Delivery and working with "Mrs. H," a 35\-year\-old pregnant woman living with severe schizophrenia who arrived in acute psychiatric crisis. Cases involving perinatal severe mental illness often test the seams of hospital systems: obstetrics, psychiatry, neonatology, and child welfare protocols all converge in a high\-stakes environment.


Watching and participating in her care reinforced why trauma\-informed communication is a clinical necessity, not an optional courtesy. By de\-escalating fear, respecting Mrs. H's humanity rather than reducing her to her diagnosis, and coordinating closely across psychiatry and OB/GYN, I saw firsthand how clinical social work anchors compassionate, ethical medical decision\-making.
