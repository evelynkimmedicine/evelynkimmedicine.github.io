**Research Citation:** Evelyn N. Kim, Coleman D. Barry, Elisa Calcagno, Elisa Zuccarello, Carlos Navedo, Steve B. Garcia Gutierrez, Erica Acquarone, & Ottavio Arancio (2025\). *Modulation of Histone H3 Acetylation at K18 and K27 by EZ115HAT: Implications for Memory Processes in Alzheimer’s Disease Treatment.* Taub Institute for Research on Alzheimer’s Disease and the Aging Brain, Columbia University.



> "Restoring epigenetic plasticity via targeted activation of histone acetylation at H3K18 and H3K27 rescues synaptic gene transcription essential for long\-term memory consolidation in Alzheimer's models."


Conducted in the laboratory of Dr. Ottavio Arancio at Columbia University's Taub Institute, this research examines how the novel small\-molecule histone acetyltransferase (HAT) activator **EZ115HAT** modulates specific lysine residues (K18 and K27\) on Histone H3 to counteract epigenetic repression in Alzheimer's Disease.
