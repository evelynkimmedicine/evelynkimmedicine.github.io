**Project Archive:** [Brain Tumor MRI Classification — Neuro\-Analysis Portfolio](https://medevelynkim.wordpress.com/neuro-analysis-projects/)


In neurosurgical oncology, rapid and accurate differentiation between primary intracranial tumors on Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) guides surgical resection planning and patient counseling. This project developed an automated classification pipeline across four clinical classes:




glioma\_tumor
Glial Parenchymal


meningioma\_tumor
Dural / Extra\-Axial


pituitary\_tumor
Sellar Region


no\_tumor
Healthy Control


**Pipeline & Clinical Relevance:** Performed skull\-stripping preprocessing, intensity normalization, data augmentation, and confusion\-matrix evaluation to minimize false\-negative classifications in high\-grade gliomas.
