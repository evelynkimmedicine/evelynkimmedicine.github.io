[https://doi.org/10\.1002/alz.078491](https://doi.org/10.1002/alz.078491)


This publication in *Alzheimer's & Dementia* synthesizes our multi\-year investigation into how soluble tau oligomers—the most synaptotoxic tau species in Alzheimer's pathology—impair the Axon Initial Segment (AIS) and disrupt action potential fidelity in cortical networks.
