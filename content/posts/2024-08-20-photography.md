> "To me, photography and clinical social work share the exact same heartbeat: looking past the surface presentation to honor the quiet, complex human story underneath—the picture underneath the picture."


Growing up in a lower\-income military family meant moving frequently across new towns, schools, and communities. Before I always had the words to belong in a new place, I had a camera. Observing people with warmth and attentiveness became my way of connecting across distance.


Today, whether capturing candid street portraits at golden hour or sitting beside a neurosurgery patient's hospital bed in New York City, that same philosophy guides my work: slowing down long enough to notice dignity, resilience, and vulnerability in every frame.
