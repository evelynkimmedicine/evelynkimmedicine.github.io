(2023\). Extracellular Tau Oligomers Damage the Axon Initial Segment. *Journal of Alzheimer's Disease*, 93(4\): 1425–1441\. [https://doi.org/10\.3233/JAD\-221284](https://doi.org/10.3233/JAD-221284)



> "Exposure of wild\-type cortical neurons to soluble extracellular tau oligomers induces significant reduction in Axon Initial Segment length and Ankyrin\-G / BetaIV\-Spectrin density—a mechanism completely prevented in tau\-knockout neurons."


**Core Experimental Contributions:**


- Quantified AIS structural markers (Ankyrin\-G and βIV\-Spectrin) across thousands of imaged neurons using automated confocal image analysis pipelines.
- Demonstrated that extracellular tau oligomers require endogenous intracellular tau to trigger AIS disassembly.
- Provided a direct cellular mechanism linking prion\-like tau propagation to network hyperexcitability and cognitive decline in Alzheimer's Disease.
