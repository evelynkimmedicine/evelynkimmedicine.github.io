**Poster Citation:** Kim, N., & Bloom, G.S. (2023\). *Tracking Alzheimer’s Disease Progression Through Multiplex IHC Biomarker Analysis.* Poster presented at the Virginia Section of ACS and UVA Department of Chemistry Undergraduate Research Symposium.


Single\-marker histology often misses the spatial interplay between multiple pathological hallmarks in Alzheimer's Disease. By optimizing multiplex immunohistochemistry (IHC) panels, this work enabled simultaneous visualization of phosphorylated tau species, cytoskeletal breakdown, and microglial activation within the same tissue section.
