> "Modern medicine is trained relentlessly in the art of 'doing'—another scan, another line, another procedure. Palliative care taught me the courage of 'being'—holding space when cure gives way to comfort and dignity."


Shadowing a palliative care social worker during family goals\-of\-care meetings for a patient ("Coby") reshaped how I envision my future as a physician. In acute surgical settings, stopping aggressive intervention can sometimes feel to families like giving up. Yet through gentle, honest facilitation, palliative care reframes the conversation around who the patient is, what brings them peace, and how to protect their dignity.
