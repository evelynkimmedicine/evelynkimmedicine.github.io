Mission Statement: Bridging Neurosurgery & Clinical Social Work

Evelyn Kim has a Columbia MSW and is an LCSW specializing in Advanced Clinical Practice with a focus on Health, Mental Health, and Disabilities. With a Bachelor’s degree in Biochemistry from the University of Virginia and an extensive background in Alzheimer’s Disease molecular research, she currently serves as a clinical intern in the Neurosurgery Department at Mount Sinai West Hospital while serving as a U.S. Air Force Medic (NREMT, LPN).

Her research and clinical work center on integrating social work principles into surgical and neurological medicine to improve holistic patient care—particularly for older adults navigating neurocognitive disorders (such as Alzheimer’s Disease) and aging immigrants facing language and systemic healthcare barriers. She aspires to become a physician-neurosurgeon who unites technical surgical precision with trauma-informed, culturally humble patient advocacy.
